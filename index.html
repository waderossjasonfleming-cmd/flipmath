The “Facebook charges 5%” myth

Search any flipping forum and you'll see “Facebook Marketplace takes 5%.” That's a US number from years ago — and it never applied to local pickup. In Canada, the reality today: 10% on shipped sales through Meta's checkout (minimum $0.80), $0 for local pickup. Read the full Facebook Marketplace Canada fee breakdown with worked examples. If you hand the buyer the item in a parking lot, you keep every dollar.

The sneaky one: sales tax on the fee

Facebook, Poshmark, Etsy and Kijiji's paid tiers add GST/HST on top of their own fee. In Ontario that's another 13% of the fee. On a $300 Poshmark sale: 20% = $60 commission, plus $7.80 HST on it. Most napkin-math sellers forget this line and wonder why the payout is short. FlipMath adds it automatically based on your province.

Worked example: one iPad, four very different payouts

You buy an iPad Air for $120 and it sells for $320 with $13 shipping. In Ontario, after fees, tax-on-fees and item cost (the $13 shipping passes through — buyer pays it, you pay the label), you actually keep:

Frequently asked

Does Facebook Marketplace charge 5% in Canada?

No. Shipped sales with Meta checkout cost 10% (minimum $0.80) in Canada as of October 2026. Local pickup sales are free. The 5% figure is an old US fee that no longer applies.

What are eBay's seller fees in Canada right now?

eBay.ca charges a 13.6% final value fee on most categories (15.3% for books, music and movies; 13.25% for trading cards, comics and coins) plus a $0.40 per-order fee for orders over $10 ($0.30 otherwise). You get 250 free listings per month, then insertion costs $0.30 each. GST/HST is added on the fee — and the percentage is charged on the shipping and sales tax your buyer paid too. Read the full eBay.ca fee breakdown with a worked example.

Is Vinted really free for sellers in Canada?

Yes — Vinted Canada charges sellers $0. The buyer pays a protection fee (~5% + $0.70). You ship with Vinted's prepaid label, and the label cost is deducted from your payout or paid by the buyer depending on your listing choice.

Is Kijiji really free to sell on?

In the buy/sell categories, yes: no commission and free listings. Kijiji only charges for featured/promoted ads, and separately for vehicles, jobs, real estate and services.

Which Canadian platform has the lowest fees?

For local pickup deals: Kijiji and Facebook local are $0. For shipped fashion: Vinted ($0 seller fee). For everything shipped outside fashion, Facebook Marketplace at 10% usually beats eBay.ca's 13.6% — but eBay often delivers higher sale prices and faster turnover, which is why you compare net, not fee percentage.

Who pays for shipping?

On Poshmark and Vinted, the platform's prepaid label is included (buyer typically pays the shipping line). On eBay, Facebook and Depop you arrange shipping — price it into the item or charge the buyer. The calculator has a shipping field for exactly this.
