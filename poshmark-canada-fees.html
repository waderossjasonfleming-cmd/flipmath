Poshmark fees in Canada: it's not 20% — it's 20% plus HST

Poshmark is where a lot of Canadian clothing resellers start, and the fee surprises people twice: first it's a full 20% of the sale, then Poshmark adds GST/HST on top of the fee. In Ontario, a $100 sale costs you $22.60 — not the $20 most sellers quote. And on small sales, a flat $3.95 fee (plus tax) can swallow a third of your price.

The small-sale trap

That flat $3.95 is the part nobody mentions. Below $20 you pay the flat fee plus tax on it — at a $15 sale that's $4.46 gone, or nearly 30 cents on the dollar. A $10 sale loses almost 45%. If most of your closet is in the $10–$18 range, Poshmark is quietly your most expensive platform:

On fees alone, Poshmark loses to Vinted (free for sellers in Canada) every time. What Poshmark does have: a bigger North American buyer base, bundle offers, and shoppers trained to pay closer to asking price for brands. If your item is a $200+ brand piece, 20% may still net you more total dollars than a $0-fee platform where it sits unsold. When Poshmark still makes sense

  • Brand-name clothing at $40 and up — the flat 20% beats marketplaces that charge on shipping when your item is light and the buyer pays the label.
  • Crosslisting strategy — list everywhere, accept that Poshmark's fee is the "convenience tax" when it sells there first.
  • Avoid for cheap items — anything under $20 belongs on Vinted or Kijiji, where $0 fees keep the margin alive.
Does Poshmark Canada charge the fee on shipping?

No. The buyer pays the Poshmark shipping label at checkout; your fee is 20% (or the $3.95 flat) of the item price alone. This is why Poshmark can beat shipped-Facebook for light, high-price items — Facebook's 10% is charged on the buyer total including shipping.

Do I pay the HST or does the buyer?

You do — the seller. Poshmark charges GST/HST on its commission, billed to you. In Ontario that turns "$20 fee" into $22.60. Most fee calculators (and most YouTube advice) leave this out.

What about Poshmark's 20% — is it negotiable?

No. There is no seller subscription that lowers it in Canada the way some US tools advertise. Your only lever is price: raise your ask by the fee difference when crosslisting.

Is Vinted really free for sellers in Canada?

Yes — Vinted Canada charges sellers $0 and adds a buyer protection fee (~5% + $0.70) to the buyer's side. Sellers can also enable an optional "shipping discount" they fund. Base listing costs you nothing.
