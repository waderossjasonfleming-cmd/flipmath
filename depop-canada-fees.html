Depop fees in Canada: "no selling fees" was never your news

In 2024 Depop announced it was scrapping its selling fee — and that story made every reseller news feed. What the headlines skipped: the removal was for US and UK sellers only. Canadian sellers still pay a ~10% selling fee on the item plus shipping, and payment processing of roughly 3.3% + $0.45 on top. If you priced your Depop closet like a US seller, you've been giving away a tenth of every sale.

Mid-pack on fees. Depop's real case isn't the fee — it's the buyer: streetwear, sneakers, Y2K vintage, and a younger audience that pays hype prices. A $120 vintage tee that sits unsold on Vinted can move in a week on Depop. The platform premium question is always "does Depop's audience pay more than Depop's fee costs?" — and that's per-item, not per-platform.

When Depop is the right platform

  • Streetwear, sneakers, Y2K and hype fashion. Depop's buyer culture literally prices items differently — expect higher asks than Vinted for the same piece.
  • Styled, personality-driven listings. Depop rewards grid-aesthetic photos and emoji-rich descriptions; if you enjoy content creation, your effort converts here.
  • Younger demographic arbitrage. Source from thrift bins and estate sales, sell to a market that never visits Value Village — that's the classic Depop flip, and the ~13% all-in fee is the toll.
  • Avoid for commodity items. Generic household goods, tools, electronics — no hype premium, no reason to pay Depop rates. Use Kijiji or Facebook.
How do I actually check what Depop Canada is charging me?

The reliable number is the deduction on a real sale, in the app, under your earnings tab — not marketing pages. Sell something cheap, check the exact deduction, and plug that percentage into your pricing. Fee labels in-app also show the split between selling fee and payment processing at checkout.

Does Depop charge on shipping or just the item?

The selling fee is calculated on the item plus shipping when the buyer pays shipping through the app. If you ship outside Depop's label system, the fee base can differ — but shipping off-platform costs you seller protection. Assume fees on your full buyer total when pricing.

Should I just sell everything on Vinted instead?

Vinted is $0 for Canadian sellers, so for pure commodity fashion it dominates Depop. But fee percentage is only half the equation — price realized and sale speed matter more. Crosslist both, track what actually sells where, and let your own data decide. That's exactly what FlipMath's comparison tab computes per item.

What about Depop's buyer fees — do they affect me?

Buyer-side fees shape what buyers will bid, which shapes your ask. If your buyer sees protection fees at checkout, their offer drops accordingly. Watch your category's realized prices, not just your fee schedule.
