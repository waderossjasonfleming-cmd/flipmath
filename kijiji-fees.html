Kijiji selling fees: $0 commission — the money math nobody explains

Kijiji is the last big Canadian marketplace where the deal is actually free: $0 commission, $0 listing fee, no checkout markup in buy/sell categories. You and the buyer settle in cash or e-Transfer at pickup, and Kijiji never touches the money. That makes it the cheapest place to sell in Canada — if the item is the kind people buy locally.

The trade: no fees, but you carry the risk and the legwork

  • No seller protection. No platform checkout means no platform refund policy — and no protection against buyer scams. The classic Kijiji scam is an "overpayment" e-Transfer followed by a reversal request. Never ship for a Kijiji buyer, never accept more than the agreed price, and prefer cash.
  • Meeting strangers is the cost of $0. Busy public spots, daylight, a friend nearby if the item is expensive. Safe-exchange zones at police stations exist in many Canadian cities for exactly this.
  • Your price is your negotiation. Expect haggling culture — most Kijiji buyers open 20–30% low. Price with room to come down, or list firm and wait for the right buyer.

When Kijiji is the right platform

  • Bulky and local. Furniture, appliances, treadmills, desks, patio sets — items where shipping would cost more than the margin. Local pickup is the only model that works, and Kijiji has the deepest local buyer pool in Canada.
  • Speed over price. A $0 listing with same-day cash pickup beats a 7-day eBay auction at higher fees when you want the item gone this weekend.
  • Testing the local market. Free relisting means you can test asks by neighborhood and pull real local comps before pricing an eBay listing.

Does Kijiji take a cut when I sell?

No. In buy/sell categories there is no success fee, no payment processing fee, no payout fee — because Kijiji isn't in the payment path at all. The sale is between you and the buyer.

Are promoted ads worth paying for?

For high-value items in crowded categories, a bump can be worth it — the math is simple: if a few dollars of promotion speeds up a $200 sale you'd make anyway, it's positive EV. For cheap items, skip it; reposting with fresh photos and a sharper title is free and often works just as well.

Can I ship items sold on Kijiji?

Technically you can arrange shipping privately with a buyer, but Kijiji explicitly warns against it and you lose every protection. Kijiji is a local-pickup platform; if you want to ship, use eBay, Facebook shipped checkout, or Vinted instead.

How many free listings do I get?

Buy/sell listings are free in normal quantities; Kijiji limits how many identical or active ads one account runs (limits vary by category) to discourage spam. Deleting old ads and varying your listings keeps you well inside them.
