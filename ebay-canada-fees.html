eBay.ca fees in Canada: 13.6% is only the start

Most Canadian sellers know eBay takes "about 13.6%." What bites them is everything stacked on top: the fee applies to shipping and tax too, there's a per-order fee on top of the percentage, and then HST/GST is added to the fee itself. Here is the full stack, verified October 2026 from eBay.ca's published fee schedule.

The percentage is applied to the entire buyer payment — item price, shipping charge, and sales tax — not just the item.

The trap: the fee applies to your shipping too

You sell a vintage game console for $100 and charge $13 shipping. eBay's 13.6% isn't $13.60 — it's calculated on the full order. In Ontario with 13% HST added to the fee, a $100 + $13-shipping order costs you about $20.08 in fees — the fee applies to the shipping the buyer paid and to the tax eBay collected too before you've paid for the label. The number most sellers quote ("13.6%") is the starting rate, not the effective one.

When eBay.ca is still the right choice

  • Reach and price — collectibles, electronics, and niche items often fetch 20–50% more on eBay than a local platform; the fee is worth paying when the price gap is bigger than the fee gap.
  • Media and cards — 15.3% and 13.25% categories still beat Poshmark's flat 20% on most price points.
  • Auction format — eBay is the only major platform where competitive bidding can push your price past any listing.
  • Buyer trust — money-back-guarantee buyers pay premium prices, especially on phones and consoles.

Rule of thumb: eBay wins when the item is shippable, searchable, and collectible. Local platforms win when the item is bulky, cheap to hand over in person, or low-value (skip the $0.40 per-order fee on $10 items — it's a 4% surcharge at that price point).

eBay.ca fee FAQ

Why is my payout less than 13.6% math predicted?

Three stacked reasons: (1) the 13.6% applies to item + shipping + tax, not just the item; (2) the per-order fee ($0.40) is added on top; (3) GST/HST is charged on the total fee in most provinces. Effective all-in fee on a $100 + $13-shipping Ontario order is about 20% of your item price, not 13.6%.

Does the per-order fee apply per item or per order?

Per order — a buyer purchasing 3 items in one combined order pays one $0.40 fee. This is one reason offering combined shipping genuinely changes your economics.

Is the 15.3% media category fee worth avoiding?

Only if the alternative platform's rate is lower for that item. Books and media often sell for more on eBay than on Kijiji or Facebook local — compare net, not fee percentage. The calculator does this in one screen.

Do I pay the fee on the tax the buyer pays?

Yes — the final value fee is calculated on the full amount including sales tax. It feels unfair; it's still the rule. Budget for it.
