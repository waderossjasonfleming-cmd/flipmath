# FlipMath Canada 🇨🇦

**Free profit & fee calculator for Canadian resellers** — know what you *actually* keep after fees, shipping, and HST on 8 selling platforms.

**Live:** https://waderossjasonfleming-cmd.github.io/flipmath/

- **Where to sell** — net profit side-by-side on eBay.ca, Facebook Marketplace (shipped *and* local pickup), Kijiji, Poshmark Canada, Etsy Canada, Vinted Canada, and Depop
- **What to pay** — maximum buy price for your target margin, before you overpay at a yard sale
- **Break-even & ROI** — the real number, with HST/GST on fees included (the part most calculators skip)

## Why it exists

US fee calculators are wrong for Canada. The "5% Facebook fee" quoted everywhere is an American number — Canadian sellers pay 10% shipped (min $0.80), $0 for local pickup. Poshmark CA charges 20% + GST/HST *on the fee itself*. These differences turn "great deal" into "silent loss."

All rates verified **October 18, 2026** from the platforms' own fee pages. When they change, the site gets updated.
