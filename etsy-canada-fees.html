Etsy fees in Canada: three fees stacked on the same dollar

Etsy never charges you one fee — it charges three, and they stack on the same dollar. Listing ($0.27), transaction (6.5%), processing (3% + $0.25) — and the percentages apply to your item plus shipping. Then GST/HST goes on top of the fees themselves. On a $100 Ontario sale with $13 shipping, that's $12.72 in fees plus tax on them, before you've paid for materials or the label.

Offsite Ads, if it applies to your sale, adds its percentage on top — verify current rate and thresholds in your shop dashboard before opting in.

On fees alone, Etsy sits mid-pack — cheaper than eBay and Poshmark, pricier than a local cash deal. What you're buying for those fees is buyer intent: Etsy shoppers arrive looking for handmade, vintage, and craft-supply items with a credit card ready. For the right inventory, Etsy's smaller fee plus higher willingness-to-pay beats every alternative.

When Etsy is the right platform

  • Handmade and craft. Etsy's buyer base expects handcrafted prices — the 12.7% all-in fee is a cost of doing business in a marketplace built for it.
  • True vintage (20+ years). Vintage is an official Etsy category with demand that eBay's auction culture doesn't replicate — collectors browse, they don't bid.
  • Craft supplies in bulk. Fabric lots, beads, patterns — small flat-rate parcels with repeat buyers.
  • Not for generic resale. Mass-produced goods and dropshipping violate Etsy's handmade policy and the buyers punish it in reviews. Resellers belong on eBay, Poshmark, Vinted or Facebook.
Does "free shipping" save me fees?

No — it can cost you slightly more. The percentages follow the money: build $13 shipping into a $113 price and you pay 6.5% + 3% on the whole $113 either way. Free shipping is a conversion lever, not a fee lever.

What happens if my listing expires unsold?

After four months it expires and you can renew for another $0.27. Slow-moving inventory accumulates listing fees — renew strategically, not automatically. If an item has expired twice with no hearts or views, that's data: your price or your photos need work.

Do I need a business number or can I sell as a person?

Etsy doesn't require a business number to open a shop, but Canadian sellers are responsible for their own tax situation — once you cross hobby-scale revenue, GST/HST registration may apply. That's a CRA question, not an Etsy one; many Canadian resellers track income in a simple P&L and talk to an accountant at year-end.

How do returns work on Etsy?

Return policies are set by the seller, not the platform, but Etsy can step in on cases under its Purchase Protection program. If a case is decided against you, you refund and the fees are returned — the sale unwinds. Clear photos and honest condition notes prevent nearly all of it.
